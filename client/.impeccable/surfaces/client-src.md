# Client: Twój Tajny Mikołaj

Scope: the whole Vue client (`client/src`). Mode: Operate for the event screens (dashboard, wishlists, kids, claims, assignment, manage), Persuade for the landing page. Built code-led (no image generation).

Audience and job: see PRODUCT.md. The organizer gets an event through participants → invitations → draw; participants, older relatives included, find out whom they buy for and what that person wants.

Direction: paper and stationery, pinned by the user after the 2026-10-17 critique. It is based on Polish gift wrapping: szary papier (kraft) tied with cranberry twine, gift tags and sealed envelopes. Memorable moment: unsealing your gift tag.

Unresolved: whether the organizer's draw should become a shared ceremony (raised in the critique, not decided).

## Direction contract

THESIS: The secret is a physical object. The assignment is a sealed gift tag, the draw produces envelopes, and the event is a sheet of paper laid on wrapping paper. Refuses the centred hero slab, the 3-card step grid and the identical white cards on cream.

OWN-WORLD: The ground is sand-brown wrapping paper (#d8c3a5). Content sits on crisp off-white paper sheets with a small radius and a soft shadow, as if lying on a table. The shell is a deep pine band. Cranberry carries the twine, primary actions, wax seals and the revealed name itself (as contrast-safe ink); gold appears only on the seal and as the foil rule under the revealed name. Headings use Półtawski Nowy, the Polish national antiqua; body text uses Atkinson Hyperlegible Next. Dividers are dashed rules, never nested panels.

STORY: The organizer always sees which of the three steps comes next. Participants open a tag that stays sealed until they choose to reveal it, so the secret survives the family table.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Landing: on kraft, a left column with a large Półtawski headline, lead and cranberry CTA; on the right, a large example gift tag tied with twine for "Wigilia u Kowalskich", labelled as an example. Assignment: below the event header, a sealed tag at the centre with the reveal button on it; the wishlist sheet follows.

FORM: Paper and stationery, pinned by the user (position 1 of 1). seed: none (user-pinned; concept-seed not run). Disclosed exception: the user pinned this direction in the critique round, and a user-pinned direction beats the roll.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
