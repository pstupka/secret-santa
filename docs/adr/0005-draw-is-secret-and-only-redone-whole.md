# Assignments are visible only to the giver; the draw is redone, never edited

Nobody, including the organizer, can see assignments other than their own (a caretaker also sees their managed participant's). Therefore once drawn, membership and exclusions are locked, and the only way to change anything is to redo the whole draw, which re-emails everyone. Editing individual pairs would require someone to see them. Caretaker and managed participant are always excluded from drawing each other, since the caretaker would otherwise learn their own Santa.
