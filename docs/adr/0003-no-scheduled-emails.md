# No scheduled emails; every email is triggered by a user action

The original idea had a scheduled "initial mail from Santa" date and post-exchange messages. Instead, the organizer builds the participant list silently and sends invitations explicitly (in bulk to the not-yet-invited, or to one participant), and the draw-result email is sent when the organizer runs the draw. There is no scheduler for emails, no reminder emails, and no "initial mail" date on the event. This removes timezone-sensitive background sends and gives the organizer full control over when relatives get contacted.
